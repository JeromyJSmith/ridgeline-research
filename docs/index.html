How to read this. Every rule carries a citation status: P means the section was read verbatim from primary text, S means a secondary source, and U means it could not be verified — those are leads, not citations, and are badged as such throughout. Six claims encountered during the research were withdrawn as unsourced; they are listed in the Method tab rather than quietly dropped.
A caveat that matters commercially. Every permission cited here is edition-dependent, so the addressable market is literally a code-adoption map. And connected monitoring has not historically deleted mandated inspections — eight years of connected elevator monitoring removed no required ASME inspection. Both facts are kept visible rather than argued around.
