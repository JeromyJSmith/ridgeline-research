The supervision discount nobody sells

NFPA 25 has for decades granted frequency relief wherever a condition is electrically supervised and reported to a constantly attended location — no AHJ approval, no listing, no performance-based programme. The hard condition is the constantly attended location. A hotel front desk is staffed 24/7, so it is satisfied for free.

The impairment clock

Compensating measures trigger past 10 hours out of service in any 24 for water-based systems (NFPA 25 §15.5.2(4)) and 4 hours for fire alarm (NFPA 101 §9.6.1.8). The window is cumulative, not per-event — three separate 4-hour outages in one day cross the 10-hour line. The signature boundary

Every certification block is a first-person attestation bound to a named individual and a licence number. The machine produces the evidence and pre-fills the form; a licensed human signs. Modelled as its own object so the boundary lives in the data, not in the copy.

Method, and what was thrown away

Seven parallel research lanes, ~450 sourced URLs, ~653KB of dossiers. Precision on section numbers was prioritised over breadth.

The paywall unlock. NFPA standards are paywalled and block automated retrieval — but NFPA publishes its committee First and Second Draft reports free and un-gated at docinfofiles.nfpa.org, and those reports reproduce the published prior edition verbatim as the baseline for proposed changes. That yielded primary text for the 2022 and 2025 editions of NFPA 72. Public.Resource.Org hosts full text of standards incorporated by reference into law.
